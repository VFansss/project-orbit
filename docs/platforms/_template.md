## Golden preservation standards

Explain what are the format that Orbit considers "golden" for preservation, and explain why

A preservation golden standard is a format that:

- Readable by most of the emulatore/platform without any hassles
- Can save as much as space as possible, without noticeable speed or functionality loss
- Lossless conversion
- It can always be reverted back to original format
