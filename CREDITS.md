### Libretro / RetroArch System BIOS Database
- **Resource ID:** `libretro-system-bios`
- **Provider:** Libretro Team & Community Contributors
- **Direct Resource Link:** [System.dat (Raw Download)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/libretro/libretro-database/master/dat/System.dat)
- **Repository Link:** [libretro/libretro-database](https://github.com/libretro/libretro-database)
- **Description:** Comprehensive checksum database (CRC32, MD5, SHA1) for system firmware and BIOS files.
